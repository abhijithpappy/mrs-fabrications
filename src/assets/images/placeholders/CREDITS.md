# Placeholder photography — replace before launch

Every image in this folder is a **temporary royalty-free stock photo** from
[Unsplash](https://unsplash.com) (Unsplash License: free to use, no attribution
required). They illustrate the *type* of work only. They are **not** photographs
of MRS Fabrications' own projects and must not be presented as such.

The website labels every image from this folder with a small
"Representative image" tag automatically (see `src/lib/placeholders.ts`).
Once you move to real photos stored elsewhere (e.g. `src/assets/images/projects/`),
the labels disappear on their own.

## How to replace

1. Put your real photos in `src/assets/images/projects/<project-name>/` (projects)
   or `src/assets/images/site/` (hero, about, workshop…).
2. Update the image paths in the project's Markdown file (`src/content/projects/`)
   or in `src/data/media.ts`.
3. When nothing references a placeholder any more, delete it from this folder.
