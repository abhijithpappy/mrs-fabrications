A good door is one you never have to think about. We make aluminium doors that open easily, close firmly and look right in the space — from glazed balcony doors to practical bathroom and utility doors.

Each door is made to the measured opening, with the frame, panel type and hardware chosen around how the door will be used. Glass panels can be clear to share light or frosted where you need privacy.

We install and adjust every door on site so it swings and latches properly, then seal the frame for a neat, weather-tight finish.
