Windows shape how a room feels — how much light comes in, how air moves and how the outside is framed. We fabricate aluminium windows to the exact size of each opening and install them with careful attention to sealing, so they look sharp and stay weather-tight.

Whether you are building new or replacing old timber or steel windows, we start with a site visit to measure every opening and talk through the style, glass and ventilation you need. Frames are then cut and assembled in the workshop and installed by our team.

Every installation is finished with proper sealing around the frame, smooth-running hardware and a final check of each sash before we hand over.
