Sliding systems are the simplest way to open a room up. Large glazed panels glide along their tracks, connecting living spaces to sit-outs, balconies and gardens while taking up no extra floor space.

Because sliding panels are large and heavy, precision matters. We measure each opening carefully, fabricate frames square and true, and set up tracks and rollers so the panels move smoothly and seal properly when closed.

We can add mesh shutters for ventilation without insects, and locks and handles that suit how the door is used.
