Partitions let you create rooms without building walls. A well-made glass and aluminium partition keeps a space bright and open, sounds better than an open floor and looks far more refined than a plywood cabin.

We plan partitions around how the space is used — where people walk, where privacy matters and where light comes from. Frames are fabricated to measure and installed with straight lines, tidy joints and well-fitted doors.

The same approach works at home: bathroom and shower partitions, room dividers and glazed screens.
