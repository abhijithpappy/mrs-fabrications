Large sliding doors change how a home is lived in. With the panels open, the living room and the garden become one space; closed, the glass still frames the view.

Because the panels are big and heavy, the opening needs to be measured precisely and the tracks set perfectly level. Each panel is then adjusted so it glides easily and seals tightly when shut.
