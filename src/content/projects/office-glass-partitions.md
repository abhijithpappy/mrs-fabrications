Glass partitions are one of the quickest ways to turn an open floor into a working office. Cabins and meeting rooms get the privacy they need, while daylight still reaches the centre of the floor.

A typical office project begins with a layout discussion: where teams sit, which rooms need privacy, and where doors should open. Partition frames are fabricated to measure, then installed in a sequence planned to keep disruption to a minimum.
