A whole-house glazing package brings every window and door into one consistent design: matching frame colours, aligned sightlines and glass chosen for each room's light and privacy.

Work like this starts with measuring every opening on site and agreeing the layout of fixed and opening panels. Frames are then fabricated in batches in the workshop and installed floor by floor, with careful sealing to keep out monsoon rain.
